Operating legally

Built on trust and compliance

LocalLoop operates as a marketplace connecting independent professionals with customers. We do not employ service providers. All activity complies with Maltese and EU law.

GDPR Compliant

All data handling is fully EU GDPR compliant, under the supervision of the local data protection authority.

Provider Self-Employment

Providers operate as registered sole traders or businesses. They manage their own tax, social security (Class 2), and VAT obligations under Maltese law.

Marketplace, Not Employer

LocalLoop is an intermediary platform under Maltese and EU Digital Services Act frameworks. We connect parties — we do not direct the work.

Consumer Protection

Our terms and dispute resolution processes align with EU consumer protection standards.

Important note for service providers

Using LocalLoop does not replace your legal obligations as a self-employed professional. If you earn income through the platform, you are responsible for registering with Jobsplus, filing annual tax returns with the Commissioner for Revenue (CFR), and paying Social Security Contributions (Class 2, approximately 15% of net profit). VAT registration is mandatory once annual turnover exceeds €30,000. LocalLoop does not act as your employer. We recommend consulting a Maltese accountant to ensure full compliance.
